VimbisoPay.Africa is a Zimbabwean/Canadian startup that solves small change and payments problems with a simple WhatsApp chatbot and Android app.

How to get started:

  • Become a member and create a free account when you send "Hi" on WhatsApp to 263 785 304 448 or download the VimbisoPay Android app (coming soon).
  • Purchase a secured credex balance with cash or mobile money.
  • Use your free account to send up to $10/day in any amount, or upgrade to the Hustler role for $1/month to get unlimited transactions. Special offer: first 10,000 Hustlers get a year for $1.
  • When repeat customers need a few cents or a few dollars in change, send it from your balance.
  • Allow customers to use their credex balances in their future transactions to pay you change they owe.
  • You or your customers can cash your balance out any time with VimbisoPay.

VimbisoPay is a scalable solution for payments of any amount. Once you and your customers are comfortable using it, you can start accepting it for full payments, if doing so adds value to your business.

Is my money safe?

The VimbisoPay software is built with auditable transparency. The source code is public (here and here), and the VimbisoPay team guarantees that behind every dollar of your secured credex balances lies a real dollar held in trust. You can cash out at any time.
