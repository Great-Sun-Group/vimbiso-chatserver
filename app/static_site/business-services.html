Small change and payments for Zimbabwe
Free WhatsApp chatbot and Android app

How to get started:

  1. Send "Hi" on WhatsApp to 263 785 304 448 to become a member.
  2. Purchase a secured credex balance with cash or mobile money.
  3. When your customers need a few cents or a few dollars in change, send it from your balance.
  4. Accept secured credex back when your customers owe you change.

Once you and your customers are comfortable using VimbisoPay, you can start accepting it for payments of any amount. Send $10/day for free, or get unlimited transactions for $1/month.

Is my money safe?

The VimbisoPay software is built with auditable transparency. The source code is public (here and here), and the VimbisoPay team guarantees that behind every dollar of your secured credex balances lies a real dollar held in trust. You can cash out at any time.
